Make a picture puzzle.

Hypnic Teen has two guessing games — Guess the Song and Guess the Movie. In them, everybody is shown a few pictures and has to work out the name from them. What you make here is one of those puzzles.

How it works

Pick a song or a film. Then choose photos that spell out its name — not photos of it. It is like a rebus, or the Bioscope round on TV.

For example, for the song Kaakka Kaakka
🐦a crow
kaakka + 🐦another crow
kaakka = Kaakka Kaakka
Or for the film The Lion King
🦁a lion + 👑a crown
king = The Lion King

The picture can stand for a word, a sound, or part of one. Anything the room will shout the answer at. Two pictures is enough; five or six is plenty.

Is it already in?

Type a song or film here first. If somebody has already made a puzzle for it, you will see it below — pick a different one and save yourself the hunt.
